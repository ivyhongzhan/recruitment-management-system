# MSc Dissertation — Methods and Materials

**Hong Zhan · University of York · MSc Social Media and Management · 2023**<br>
**Supervisor: Dr Daniel Robins**

**Dissertation:** *Differences between the Use of Social Media by Chinese Students in the UK and Their Home Country's Mainstream Media Culture*

## Research question

How does the use of social media by Chinese students studying in the UK impact their sense of cultural identity and belonging?

## What I did

I designed and conducted **15 semi-structured interviews** with Chinese students in the UK. Interviews lasted **30–60 minutes**. I recorded, transcribed, translated, and organized the materials, then used **NVivo** for thematic coding and **Excel** for organization. I also analyzed social-media posts to examine participation and information evaluation.

The analysis identified platform-specific communication practices, language barriers, and cross-platform credibility checking. It also considered perceived differences in platform norms and content restrictions. These themes describe participants' experiences, rather than population prevalence or causal effects.
